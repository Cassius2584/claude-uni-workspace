# Week 1 – Vector spaces and subspaces (EXAMPLE)

## Key ideas
- A **vector space** over a field F is a set V with addition and scalar multiplication satisfying the eight
  axioms (associativity, commutativity, identity, inverses, distributivity ×2, compatibility, unit).
- A **subspace** is a subset U ⊆ V that is itself a vector space. Test: 0 ∈ U, and U is closed under
  addition and scalar multiplication.
- The **span** of a set S is the smallest subspace containing S.

## Examples
- ℝⁿ, polynomials of degree ≤ n, continuous functions on [0, 1].
- {(x, y) : y = 2x} is a subspace of ℝ²; {(x, y) : y = 2x + 1} is not (no zero vector).
